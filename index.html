Master the Kenyan Kitchen: The Big 5 Essential Spices

In a world of complicated recipes and overflowing spice cabinets, we believe in a simpler truth: You don’t need fifty different jars to cook like a pro. You just need the right five.


At Nyumbani Fresh Blends, we’ve stripped away the noise to focus on the "Big 5"—the foundational spices that define the soul of Kenyan cooking. Whether you’re simmering a Sunday Pilau or a quick weekday Githeri, these pure, unadulterated essentials are all you need to transform a meal from basic to brilliant.

1. Ground Ginger (Tangawizi)

  • The Vibe: Warm, zingy, and energizing.
  • The Nyumbani Difference: Most commercial ginger is fibrous and pale. Ours is deep, potent, and ground from the finest sun-dried roots.
